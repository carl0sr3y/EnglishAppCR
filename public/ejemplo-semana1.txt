@lesson Semana 1 - Lección: el verbo to be
## Para qué sirve
El verbo to be une a una persona con algo: quién es, cómo está o dónde está. Es la base de casi todo lo demás en inglés.
> I am a developer. | Soy programador.
> She is an artist. | Ella es artista.
> They are in Rome. | Ellos están en Roma.
## Cómo se conjuga
- I am
- he / she / it is
- you / we / they are
! En español puedes omitir el sujeto ("soy", "estoy"). En inglés casi nunca: siempre dices I am, she is, they are...
## Negativo y pregunta
Para el negativo agregas not. Para la pregunta pones el verbo primero.
> I am not tired. | No estoy cansado.
> She isn't here. | Ella no está aquí.
> Is she Italian? | ¿Es italiana?
> Are you ready? | ¿Estás listo?
## Por qué importa
Casi todos los tiempos que vienen después usan to be. Si lo dominas ahora, el presente continuo te saldrá casi solo.

@lesson Semana 1 - Lección: presente continuo
## Cuándo se usa
Para lo que pasa ahora mismo o en esta etapa (esta semana, este mes).
> I am painting a portrait now. | Estoy pintando un retrato ahora.
> She is building a website this week. | Ella está construyendo una página web esta semana.
## Cómo se forma
Sujeto + am / is / are + verbo con -ing.
- I am painting
- he / she / it is drawing
- you / we / they are coding
! Tu error más común: olvidar am / is / are. Nunca dices "I painting"; siempre I am painting.
## Ortografía del -ing
- Normal: paint -> painting, draw -> drawing
- Termina en -e: quita la e: code -> coding, write -> writing
- Verbo corto con vocal + consonante: duplica la consonante: run -> running
## Negativo y pregunta
> I am not working today. | Hoy no estoy trabajando.
> Are you learning Italian? | ¿Estás aprendiendo italiano?
> Is she debugging the app? | ¿Está depurando la app?

@lesson Semana 1 - Lección: presente simple o continuo
## La regla
Hábito o rutina: presente simple. Algo que pasa ahora o en esta etapa: presente continuo.
> I write code every night. | Escribo código todas las noches.
> I am writing a new feature today. | Hoy estoy escribiendo una funcionalidad nueva.
## Tercera persona (he / she / it)
El verbo lleva -s en afirmativo. En negativo usas doesn't + verbo base, sin -s.
> Marco paints in the morning. | Marco pinta por la mañana.
> She doesn't paint on Sundays. | Ella no pinta los domingos.
## Palabras clave
- Presente simple: always, usually, every day, on Mondays, never
- Presente continuo: now, right now, at the moment, today, this week
## Verbos que no llevan -ing
Los verbos de gusto o pensamiento (like, love, want, know, need) casi nunca se usan en continuo.
> I like Italian art. | Me gusta el arte italiano.
! No digas "I am liking Italian art". Di I like Italian art.

@exercises Semana 1 - Gramática A: am, is, are
I ___ painting a portrait now. => am | I + am. Con -ing siempre necesitas am/is/are.
She ___ writing code. => is | She + is.
They ___ visiting Florence this week. => are | They + are.
We ___ drawing in the gallery. => are | We + are.
He ___ not working today. => is | He + is not (isn't).
___ you learning Italian? => are | En pregunta, am/is/are va primero: Are you learning...?
My brother ___ debugging the app. => is | My brother = he, entonces is.
It ___ raining in Rome. => is | It + is.

@exercises Semana 1 - Gramática B: simple o continuo
Marco usually ___ (paint) in the morning. => paints | usually = hábito, Present Simple. He + verbo con -s.
Right now Marco ___ (paint) a landscape. => is painting | right now = Present Continuous.
I ___ (write) code every night. => write | every night = hábito. Con I, verbo base.
Today I ___ (write) a new feature. => am writing / i am writing / i'm writing | today = temporal, Present Continuous. Write pierde la e: writing.
She ___ (not like) spicy food. => doesn't like / does not like | Like es un verbo de gusto: no lleva -ing. Negativo con she = doesn't + verbo base.
They ___ (have) dinner at 8 pm every day. => have | every day = hábito, Present Simple.
At the moment he ___ (have) dinner. => is having | At the moment = ahora. Have dinner sí admite -ing.
Escribe la pregunta: you / draw / faces / this week => are you drawing faces this week | Are + sujeto + verbo-ing: Are you drawing faces this week?

@reading Semana 1 - Lectura: Giulia's two hobbies
Giulia lives in Florence, Italy. She is twenty-six years old and she works as a web developer for a small museum. Every morning she walks across the Ponte Vecchio bridge, and she always stops to look at the river.
Last year she built a website where visitors can see the museum's paintings on their phones. Before she started that project, she was learning to paint with watercolors in the evenings.
Now she is combining both hobbies: she is creating an app that turns her paintings into puzzles. She hasn't finished it yet, but she is going to launch it next spring. Her friends think it is a great idea.
? Where does Giulia work? | In a restaurant; In a small museum*; In a school; In a bank | Dice que trabaja como web developer para un museo pequeño.
? What did she build last year? | A video game; A bridge; A website for the museum's paintings*; A new painting | "Last year she built a website..."
? What was she doing in the evenings before the website project? | Learning to paint with watercolors*; Building an app; Traveling to Rome; Working at the museum | Past Progressive: was learning = estaba aprendiendo.
? What is she going to do next spring? | Close the museum; Launch her app*; Move to another city; Paint a new picture | "She is going to launch it next spring."
? "She hasn't finished it yet." What does it mean? | The app is ready; The app is still not complete*; She lost the app; She doesn't like the app | Yet en negativo = todavía no.
? In the text, "combining both hobbies" means: | Separating them; Forgetting them; Joining them together*; Selling them | Combining = combinando, juntando.
