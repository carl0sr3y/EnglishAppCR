@unit A1.1 Saludos y presentaciones
desc: Saludar, despedirte, presentarte y decir de dónde eres con el verbo to be.

@lesson A1.1 - Lección: saludos y el verbo to be
## Saludar y despedirte
Con estas frases puedes empezar cualquier conversación.
> Hello! My name is Ana. | ¡Hola! Me llamo Ana.
> Nice to meet you. | Mucho gusto.
> How are you today? | ¿Cómo estás hoy?
> See you later! | ¡Hasta luego!
## El verbo to be (ser / estar)
Une a una persona con algo: quién es, de dónde es, cómo está.
- I am (yo soy / estoy)
- you are (tú eres / estás; ustedes son / están)
- he / she / it is (él, ella, eso es / está)
- we are (nosotros somos / estamos)
- they are (ellos son / están)
! En español puedes omitir el sujeto ("soy Ana"). En inglés casi nunca: siempre dices I am Ana.
## Frases completas
> I am a student. | Soy estudiante.
> She is from Spain. | Ella es de España.
> We are friends. | Somos amigos.
## Negativo y pregunta
Para el negativo agrega not. Para preguntar, pon el verbo primero.
> I am not tired. | No estoy cansado.
> He isn't at home. | Él no está en casa.
> Are you ready? | ¿Estás listo?
> Is she a doctor? | ¿Ella es médica?
## Recuerda
Para presentarte puedes decir My name is... o I am... Las dos son correctas.

@exercises A1.1 - Ejercicios: am, is o are
I ___ a student. => am | Con I siempre se usa am.
She ___ from Mexico. => is | She = ella: is.
We ___ friends. => are | We = nosotros: are.
They ___ teachers. => are | They = ellos: are.
My name ___ Ana. => is | My name es "eso": is.
You ___ very kind. => are | You siempre va con are.
He ___ not at home. => is | Negativo: is not (isn't).
___ you ready? => Are | En la pregunta el verbo va primero.
___ she a doctor? => Is | Pregunta con she: Is she...?
I ___ not tired. => am | Negativo con I: am not (I'm not).

@unit A1.2 Mi familia y mi casa
desc: Hablar de tu familia y tu casa con have / has y my, your, his, her, our, their.

@lesson A1.2 - Lección: have / has y los posesivos
## Have / has (tener)
Para decir lo que tienes. Con he, she, it se usa has; con los demás, have.
- I have
- you have
- he / she / it has
- we / they have
> I have two brothers. | Tengo dos hermanos.
> She has a big family. | Ella tiene una familia grande.
## Posesivos: de quién es
Van antes del sustantivo y no cambian por el plural.
- my (mi) · your (tu, su de usted) · his (su de él) · her (su de ella)
- our (nuestro) · their (su de ellos)
> This is my mother. | Esta es mi madre.
> That is his car. | Ese es su coche.
> They love their garden. | Ellos aman su jardín.
! Cuidado: en inglés se dice his o her según quién tiene la cosa, no según la cosa. His mother = la madre de él.
## El 's: de quién es
Para decir "de alguien" agregas 's al nombre.
> Tom's house is big. | La casa de Tom es grande.
> My sister's room is small. | El cuarto de mi hermana es pequeño.

@reading A1.2 - Lectura: Tom's family
Tom has a big family. He has two sisters and one brother. His mother is a nurse and his father is an engineer.
His grandmother lives with them. She is seventy years old. She cooks in the kitchen every Sunday.
Tom's house is not very big, but it has a nice garden. The family eats lunch in the garden when it is sunny.
? How many sisters does Tom have? | One; Two*; Three | Dice: He has two sisters.
? What is Tom's mother's job? | Nurse*; Teacher; Engineer | Dice: His mother is a nurse.
? Who lives with the family? | His grandmother*; His brother; His friend | Dice: His grandmother lives with them.
? When does the grandmother cook? | Every Sunday*; Every morning; Never | Dice: She cooks in the kitchen every Sunday.
? Where does the family eat when it is sunny? | In the kitchen; In the garden*; At school | Dice: The family eats lunch in the garden.
